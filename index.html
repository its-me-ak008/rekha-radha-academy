1. Foundation Sewing

Basic stitching, machine handling, fabric selection, and cutting skills.

2. Pattern Making

3. Garment Construction

4. Finishing & Detailing

Trims, hemming, embroidery finishing, fitting, and final presentation.

5. Boutique Skills

Pricing, customer handling, small business setup, and order management.

Create a professional collection and get ready for freelance work.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you should know before joining RR Academy

Is RR Academy beginner-friendly?

Yes. RR Academy is designed for beginners, homemakers, students, and anyone who wants to build practical tailoring skills from the ground up.

What will I learn in the tailoring course?

You will learn sewing fundamentals, pattern making, garment construction, finishing, fitting, and boutique-style business skills.

Do you offer practical training?

Yes. The training focuses on hands-on tailoring practice, live project work, and step-by-step learning to build confidence.

Can I start a tailoring business after the course?

Yes. The academy also includes business guidance, pricing ideas, and customer handling to help you begin your own work or boutique journey.

Is this a tailoring academy for women and career changers?

Absolutely. The course is ideal for students, women seeking flexible income, and people looking to switch into a creative career.

How do I contact the academy?

You can connect with us via WhatsApp for course details, admission guidance, and the next batch schedule.
