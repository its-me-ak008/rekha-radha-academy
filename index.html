Course outline

What you will learn

1. Foundation Sewing

Basic stitching, machine handling, fabric selection, and cutting skills.

2. Pattern Making

Understand body measurements and create accurate patterns for garments.

3. Garment Construction

Build kurta sets, dresses, tops, bottoms, and festive wear in class.

4. Finishing & Detailing

Trims, hemming, embroidery finishing, fitting, and final presentation.

5. Boutique Skills

Pricing, customer handling, small business setup, and order management.

6. Portfolio Prep

Create a professional collection and get ready for freelance work.
